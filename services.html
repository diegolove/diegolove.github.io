Brand Identity

Naming, mark, type system, color, voice, and the long-form rationale that holds it all together. We treat identity as a working document, not a launch artifact — the system has to ship a hundred times before it earns its place.

NamingWordmarkType systemColorVoiceBrand book

Typical deliverables

  • Naming & rationale
  • Primary & secondary marks
  • Type system & pairings
  • Color & usage rules
  • Voice & messaging
  • Identity book (PDF + print)
  • Application sheets
  • Hand-off & training

Process

  • Discovery & positioning (2 weeks)
  • Design exploration (4 weeks)
  • System build & documentation (4 weeks)
  • Application, hand-off, & ongoing care
