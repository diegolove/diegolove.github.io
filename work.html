Selected work, 2019 — present.

A small archive. Forty-some projects across brand, web, motion, and print. Use the filters below — case studies open in detail.

Atlas Climate·2025·Brand · Web

An identity for climate accountability.

Naming, mark, type system, and a multi-region digital platform. Launched in 19 countries on the same day.

Hearth & Co.·2025·Editorial · Brand

A 92-page cookbook for a Hudson Valley group.

The system extends from the books into menus, signage, and the restaurant's seasonal print program.

Voltage Mobility·2026·Web · Product

Product design for an EV charging network.

An end-to-end design system spanning consumer app, dashboard, and a small fleet of in-charger touchscreens.

Northform·2025·Web

A new digital home for a Brooklyn architecture studio.

An archive-first website built around a 22-year practice. Portfolio, press, and a working journal — all editable in-house.

Lumen Studio·2024·Brand · Motion

An identity for a film & motion company.

A naming and visual identity system that plays equally well on a 35mm film leader card and a TikTok end-screen.

Pier 47·2025·Brand · Editorial

A reborn hospitality brand for a 1928 wharf hotel.

Wordmark, signage, and a printed welcome book mailed to every guest before arrival.

Field Notes·2024·Editorial

A quarterly print publication, twelve issues in.

Long-form essays on craft, climate, and the slow life. We design and produce every issue with the editorial team.

Solis Beverages·2025·Brand · Packaging

A 12-SKU sparkling line, packaged.

Naming, pack architecture, and a primary system that holds together across grocery, e-commerce, and bar back walls.

Mercantile·2026·Brand · Digital

A private bank that doesn't look like a bank.

An identity, marketing site, and onboarding product for a new private banking practice in Brooklyn.

Plover Skincare·2026·Campaign · Motion

Spring '26 launch creative.

A six-week launch campaign across out-of-home, paid social, and a series of fifteen-second product films.
