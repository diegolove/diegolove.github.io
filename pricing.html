Good to know

Pricing questions, answered.

Is the flat rate really final?

Yes. Once you confirm your inventory and addresses, the price is locked. The only way it changes is if you add items or services after booking.

Do you charge for stairs or long carries?

No. Stairs, elevators, and longer walks from the truck are all folded into your flat quote up front.

What deposit do you take?

Zero deposit to book. You pay once the move is complete and you've checked every room.
